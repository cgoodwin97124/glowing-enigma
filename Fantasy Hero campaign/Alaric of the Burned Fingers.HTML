Description:
Alaric is 5'10 and around 161 pounds, with brown hair and eyes the color of fire. He has burn scars up and down his arms, on his back and face, and a few patches on his scalp where hair no longer grows.
BACKGROUND
Alaric was born on a farm in Willick Shire with two things: the magical sight and an insatiable curiosity about fire. He just seemed to get fire. His parents couldn't keep him from touching the cookstove -- they had a hard enough time keeping him from climbing into the thing. (He received his cognomen from his parents when he was three years old, and he hadn't -- yet -- burned his fingers. They knew it was inevitable.) They worked as hard as they could to teach him how to be responsible with it.

He very nearly burned down multiple structures into his teens. His bedroom in the family farmhouse has a scorched spot on a wall that they've just never had the time or ready funds to fix, and one of the outbuildings had to be rebuilt. Then there was the time he and his sister built a fort, with working fireplace, from a bunch of old Winterfest trees. (Somehow they managed to avoid burning that down with themselves inside it.)

At 12, his parents sent him to the Rosebridge Firewatch to keep him busy and to learn the downsides -- really, to keep him from burning down the farm. By the time he'd turned 16, he had burn scars up and down his arms, on his back, his face, and a few patches on his scalp where hair no longer grows. By then he was ready for his full apprenticeship, which everyone knew would be to the Fire Mages' Guild (their motto: "If they don't have at least a few burn scars, we don't want them") and took to fire magic as readily as he took to mundane fire and to fire protection.
PERSONALITY
Alaric loves fire. A lot. He also knows how vulnerable villages, towns, cities, and forests are to fire; what his parents weren't able to drum into him, the fire itself burned into him. His time in the Firewatch taught him how to respect fire, and his time as Tinder (which is what the Fire Mages call their apprentices) taught him how to befriend it; quite simply, fire doesn't burn him unless he wants it to. As Kindling (a journeyman fire mage) he once again works with the Rosebridge Firewatch.
QUOTE
"That one's a beauty. We gotta take it down before the whole district goes up!"
POWERS/TACTICS
He fights with fire, and he fights against it. In forest fires, he's particularly good at controlled burns of overgrowth areas before they become part of an uncontrolled blaze.
